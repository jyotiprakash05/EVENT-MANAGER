Cancellation, Refund & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Digital Service & Delivery Policy

Instant Digital Delivery: All tickets, passes, and event registrations booked through EventPro are delivered digitally. There are no physical shipments.

Upon successful payment completion via Razorpay:

  • Your booking is confirmed immediately in real-time.
  • Digital tickets containing unique QR access codes are instantly viewable and downloadable from your Attendee Dashboard.
  • A booking confirmation and digital receipt are automatically dispatched to your registered email address.

2. Cancellation Policy

Cancellations by Attendees:

  • Attendee cancellation eligibility is determined by the specific event organizer's policy as published on the event page.
  • Where cancellations are permitted, requests must be submitted at least 48 hours prior to the scheduled event start time through your dashboard or by emailing support@eventpro24.online.
  • Tickets booked for events starting in less than 24 hours are non-cancellable unless the event itself is postponed or cancelled by the organizer.

Cancellations by Organizers:

  • If an event is cancelled or permanently called off by the organizer, registered ticket holders will be entitled to a 100% full refund of the ticket price paid.
  • In case of event rescheduling or venue change, ticket holders will be given the option to either attend the rescheduled date or claim a full refund.

3. Refund Processing & Timelines

Refund Window: Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment source within 5 to 7 business days.

Details regarding refund execution:

  • Mode of Refund: All refunds are issued exclusively back to the original payment method used during checkout (e.g. Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, or UPI account).
  • Processing Steps: Once a refund request is approved, our automated banking partner (Razorpay) initiates the refund immediately. Depending on your issuing bank, the funds typically reflect within 5-7 business days.
  • Any applicable non-refundable banking/convenience charges or statutory taxes may be deducted in accordance with organizer guidelines where permitted by law.

4. Requesting a Refund

To request assistance with an event cancellation or refund, please follow these steps:

  • Send an email to support@eventpro24.online with the subject line "Refund Request - [Your Order/Ticket ID]".
  • Include your registered email address, phone number, and reason for the cancellation.
  • Our support team will review and respond to your request within 24 to 48 hours.
