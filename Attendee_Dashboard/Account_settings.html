Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get my deposit refund at the venue?

Your Proof-of-Presence smart deposit is automatically refunded to your original payment method within minutes the instant the event organizer scans your QR pass at the entrance gate.

Can I cancel my booking if my plans change?

Yes! All tickets booked on EventPro include 72h Refund Protection. Navigate to My Bookings and click "Cancel Booking" at least 72 hours before the event start time.

What happens if my group friends don't pay within 15 minutes?

If unpaid by the end of the 15-minute hold timer, reserved unconfirmed seats are automatically returned to public inventory so you never get stuck paying for missing guests.
