Frequent Organizer Questions

When do ticket collections reach my bank account?

Payouts are initiated within 24 hours (T+1) following event completion. For high-volume multi-day festivals, partial advance payouts can be scheduled by contacting admin@eventpro24.online.

What happens if the turnstile internet connection drops at the venue?

The EventPro Ticket Scanner caches authorized ticket cryptographic keys locally. Entry scans continue seamlessly offline and automatically sync with the cloud the moment connectivity resumes.

How does Proof-of-Presence Gate Deposit work for free events?

Free events often experience 40-50% drop-off. With our Gate Deposit feature, attendees pre-authorize a refundable ₹99 deposit. If they attend, they are fully refunded; if they abandon without 24h notice, the deposit covers your venue logistics.
