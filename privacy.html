Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

At EventPro Technologies ("EventPro"), we value your privacy. We collect the following types of information when you interact with our platform:

  • Personal Identification Details: Name, email address, phone number, and account passwords created via AWS Cognito.
  • Transaction Details: Payment order IDs, payment status, amount paid, and ticket references. Note that sensitive card credentials or bank passwords are securely processed by PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (Razorpay) and are never stored on our servers.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, device browser type, operating system, and interaction logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for purposes including:

  • Processing ticket purchases and delivering QR verification passes.
  • Verifying user accounts and maintaining platform security.
  • Sending transactional notifications, receipts, and event-related updates.
  • Providing customer support and resolving inquiries.
  • Preventing unauthorized activities, fraud, and system abuse.

3. Payment Security & Third Parties

All online financial transactions are processed securely through certified payment gateways such as Razorpay. Razorpay complies with the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI-DSS). We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage & Protection

Your data is securely stored within Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure utilizing industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/HTTPS in transit and AES-256 at rest) to safeguard against unauthorized access.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use session tokens and browser local storage to maintain your authenticated login session and preferences. You can configure your browser to reject cookies, though some platform features may be restricted.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal account data at any time by contacting our support team.
